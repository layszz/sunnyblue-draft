Cerritos

In Cerritos, members take part in sports and recreational activities that build fitness, confidence, and social skills. The chapter partners with local first responders for hands-on safety training and offers creative programs for self-expression. Members also go on educational outings to learn and explore together as a community.

Badminton — Every 1st and 3rd Sunday, 2:00–4:00 pm
Church in Cerritos, 12061 Del Amo Blvd, Cerritos, CA 90703

Cypress

SunnyBlue Cypress is a student-led orchestra that brings live music and joy to seniors, patients, and individuals of diverse abilities. Through inclusive performances, students foster connection, empathy, and community.
