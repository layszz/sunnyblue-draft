About SunnyBlue

Education, volunteerism, and acceptance

A high-school-led nonprofit for neurodivergent children, families, and elders in Cerritos, Cypress, and Upland.

Our vision

SunnyBlue is a dedicated non-profit organization that focuses on promoting education, volunteerism, and acceptance for neurodivergent individuals. Our mission is to empower individuals to take collective action and create sustainable solutions for a better community.

Our commitment

Our mission at SunnyBlue is to foster a sense of community, advocate for positive change, and empower neurodivergent individuals to actively participate in shaping a more inclusive future. We aim to address social issues, promote inclusivity, and build a strong, united community.

Through collaborative projects, advocacy efforts, and community engagement, we strive to create a more equitable and supportive environment where every voice is heard and valued.
