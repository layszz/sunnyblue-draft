About SunnyBlue

Building belonging through sports, music, and youth-led service

SunnyBlue is a high-school-student-led nonprofit serving neurodivergent people, people with disabilities, their families, and older adults in Cerritos, Cypress, and Upland. Through inclusive sports, music, arts, life-skills activities, and community service, we create joyful opportunities for people of all ages and abilities to connect, participate, and belong.

Our Vision

We envision connected communities where people of every age and ability are welcomed, valued, and able to participate fully. We also envision young people who make empathy, inclusion, leadership, and community service a lifelong practice.

Our Mission

Our mission is to promote inclusion, wellness, connection, and service by bringing students, families, and community members together through meaningful activities and volunteer opportunities.

We strive to:

  • Create inclusive and welcoming experiences where individuals with special needs, disabilities, and their families feel respected, accepted, and supported.
  • Promote physical, social, and emotional well-being through sports, music, arts, recreation, and other engaging activities.
  • Build peer connections and friendships through group activities and youth-led programs.
  • Develop social and independent living skills through real-life experiences such as cooking, dining out, shopping, outdoor activities, and community events.
  • Support siblings and families by creating opportunities for connection, participation, and positive coping experiences.
  • Empower young people through service by connecting students with meaningful volunteer opportunities and helping them become compassionate, responsible community leaders.
  • Give back to the community through outreach, performances, fundraising, educational programs, and service projects that support people of all ages and abilities.

Our Commitment

SunnyBlue is committed to dignity, choice, accessibility, and safety in every program. We listen to participants and families, design activities around individual strengths and needs, prepare student volunteers to serve responsibly, and work with local partners to learn and improve.

We believe community service is something we do with people, not simply for them, and that everyone has something meaningful to contribute.

Our Impact

Through sports, music, arts, recreation, life-skills activities, volunteer service, community outreach, and fundraising, SunnyBlue creates opportunities for people to:

Connect. Participate. Grow. Give Back.

Our goal is not only to serve our community today, but also to inspire young people to carry the values of kindness, inclusion, wellness, and service into the future.
