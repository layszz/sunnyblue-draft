Welcome to SunnyBlue

Empowering Neurodivergent Communities Through Education and Acceptance

SunnyBlue is a non-profit organization led by high school students dedicated to helping neurodivergent children, their families, and elders by promoting education, volunteerism, and acceptance. Our hands-on approach ensures that we work collaboratively to bring about positive change and create a more inclusive and supportive environment for all.
